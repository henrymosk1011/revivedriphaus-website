What is mobile IV hydration?
![](691e617843d1d852bb73f808_faq-arrow.svg)
Mobile IV hydration is a service where a licensed medical professional administers IV fluids, vitamins, and electrolytes directly into your bloodstream in the comfort of your home, office, hotel, or other location.
How does IV hydration work?
![](691e617843d1d852bb73f808_faq-arrow.svg)
IV therapy delivers fluids and nutrients directly into your bloodstream, bypassing the digestive system. This allows for faster absorption and quicker relief from dehydration, fatigue, illness, or hangovers.
What are the benefits of IV hydration?
![](691e617843d1d852bb73f808_faq-arrow.svg)
IV hydration can help with:
- Dehydration recovery
- Boosting energy and immunity
- Hangover relief/prevention
- Migraine and headache relief
- Athletic recovery
- Skin and beauty enhancement
- Cold and flu symptoms
Is IV hydration safe?
![](691e617843d1d852bb73f808_faq-arrow.svg)
Yes! Our treatments use medical-grade fluids and are administered by trained professionals. We conduct a brief health assessment before treatment to ensure safety.
Does IV hydration hurt?
![](691e617843d1d852bb73f808_faq-arrow.svg)
You may feel a small pinch when the needle is inserted, but most people find the process comfortable and relaxing.
