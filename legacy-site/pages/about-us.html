About us

At Revive Drip Haus, we bring hydration, wellness, and recovery directly to you; whenever and wherever. Whether you're battling fatigue, recovering from a night out, boosting athletic performance, or simply prioritizing your health, our on demand mobile IV hydration service delivers the fastest and most effective results.

Our team of licensed medical professionals combines over a decade of emergency medicine and critical care experience, along with unmatched concierge-level service by customizing treatments to meet your specific needs. Using high-quality vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, and electrolytes, we help you rehydrate, replenish, and revitalize, all in the comfort of your home, office, hotel, or event.

You call. We come. Because taking your wellness to the next level and feeling your best should always be convenient.
